## 💫 Interactive Features

1. **3D Royal Blue Wax-Sealed Envelope**:
   - Folded paper envelope in royal French blue with floral toile damask interior liner.
   - 3D Champagne gold wax seal with an embossed royal monogram (`K` 1 Year).
   - Realistic wax-breaking sound & golden beam animation upon opening.

2. **✦ Scratch to Reveal the Date ✦ (Interactive HTML5 Canvas)**:
   - Three metallic gold foil scratch cards:
     - `19` (DAY)
     - `NOVEMBER` (MONTH)
     - `2026` (THURSDAY)
   - Scratch with finger on mobile or mouse on desktop, or tap **"Scratch All / Reveal Date"** for instant golden confetti!

3. **Royal Arch Frame & Hanging Golden Lanterns**:
   - Traditional illuminated royal lanterns gently swaying beside the gilded stationery arch.
   - Elegant typography pairing classic calligraphy (`Pinyon Script`) with royal serifs (`Cinzel`, `Cormorant Garamond`).

4. **Royal Arch Evening Timeline**:
   - `9:00 PM` — Guest Arrival & Grand Welcome (Royal sharbat & welcome drinks)
   - `9:45 PM` — 🎂 1st Birthday Cake Cutting Ceremony
   - `10:30 PM` — Royal Dinner Feast & Festivities

5. **"Are You Coming or Not?" Viral Interactive Challenge**:
   - Playful interactive challenge for Chahat's friends:
     - **NO 😢**: Playful runaway button that dodges and pleads (*"Wait, think about the cake! 🍰"*, *"Chahat won't let you say no! 😤"*, etc.) until it finally surrenders!
     - **YES! 🥳**: The only responsive button, growing larger on every attempt, triggering celebration fanfare, confetti shower, and direct WhatsApp confirmation!

6. **Audio Experience**:
   - Built-in Web Audio API music box synthesizer playing a peaceful, celebratory melody with a floating vinyl player toggle.

7. **One-Click Directions & Calendar Integration**:
   - Direct Google Maps navigation to **Penchati Hall, Sonraki Bazar, Jacobabad**.
   - One-click Google Calendar & Apple/Outlook `.ICS` file download.
